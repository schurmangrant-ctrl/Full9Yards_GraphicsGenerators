# What makes a good Full 9 Yards clip

Full 9 Yards is a sports podcast: a handful of guys talking football (and
other sports), backing up takes with stats, graphs and charts. Clips go to
TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. The goal is followers, not just
views: a stranger should watch the clip and think "I like that guy's take"
and want to see the next one.

Edit this file to teach the picker your taste. It is sent to Claude every run.

## A good clip

- Is one complete take: a setup or question, a clear claim, the reason or
  stat behind it, and ideally a reaction or pushback from another host.
- Makes sense to someone who has not heard the rest of the episode.
- Hooks in the first sentence. If the best line comes after 20 seconds of
  warm-up, start closer to the good part.
- Has a strong, specific opinion ("Bo Nix is a bottom-10 starter and the
  numbers aren't close") over general chat.
- Has hosts disagreeing, laughing, or roasting each other. Chemistry sells.
- Uses a stat or chart to prove a point, especially a surprising one.
- Ends on a punchline, a mic-drop line, or a clean end of thought. Never
  in the middle of a sentence.

## Avoid

- Housekeeping, intros, sponsor reads, "like and subscribe".
- Takes that depend on something said much earlier and are not re-explained.
- Moments where hosts talk over each other so much the point is lost.
- Pure play-by-play recap with no opinion.

## Length

- Most clips 30 to 75 seconds.
- Include a few that run 61 to 90 seconds; TikTok's Creator Rewards only pays
  on videos over one minute.
- Never under 15 seconds or over 3 minutes.
